## Flagged decisions and assumptions

- [EDGE-UNCLASSIFIED SHAR-07] The spec-less edge probe could not classify SHAR-07 (status unresolved). Planner-authored edge assumptions pinned by tests here: a reconnect shorter than 3 s shows no banner; a first connect that fails is retried like a drop; a socket that dies before its offset meta arrives makes the next redial ask for a full replay (no resume); a malformed meta frame invalidates the offset (explicit full replay next time, never a silent partial).
- Wire values used here come from 05-01 and 05-02 and are pinned on both sides: MsgPong 0x04, MsgMeta 0x20, meta fields `replay` ("full" | "delta") and `offset` (number), query parameter `resume`.
- Offset rule: the client trusts its offset only after the current socket's offset meta; from then on it adds payload.length + 1 for every MsgOutput frame. Replay chunks before the meta are not counted (the meta replaces the count).
- Discretion (reconnect UI): the reconnecting banner reuses SessionEndedBanner as a new kind with fixed copy and no dismiss button. No tab or sidebar indicator: App mounts the web-session view only for the active tab, so an inactive peer tab holds no connection to reconnect, and the banner on the active one is the only signal needed.
- Discretion (give-up policy): retry forever with the 30 s cap; only a final close or a stop verdict from the re-auth hook ends the loop.
- Every client now pings every RECONNECT.pingMs (15 s, was 30 s); harmless on loopback and required for the 40 s dead window.
