## Phase Goal

Users navigate everything from tabs plus a sidebar: the Hub grid is gone, and the sidebar lists local sessions and tailnet peers' sessions, each opening in a tab. (ROADMAP Goal, verbatim. It is not in user-story form and no story is invented here.)

This plan is the native-chrome spike the rest of the phase builds on (RESEARCH Pitfall 1, orchestrator resolved default 1). It covers the LOOK-01 "unified title bar and toolbar" half of ROADMAP success criterion 8 and wires the menu bar.

<objective>

Purpose: CONTEXT ▸ Window & Chrome (hidden-inset title bar with traffic lights in the sidebar strip; native vibrancy behind the sidebar only; toolbar and terminal opaque) and UI-SPEC "Window chrome contract" and "Interaction Contract ▸ Menu bar". Every later plan puts its toolbar controls in this strip, so the click-through risk is retired first.
Output: main.go window options and menu, menu_test.go, desktop-only CSS for transparency and drag regions, menu listeners in App.tsx, the banner stack moved under the tab bar, TESTING.md rows and manual items.
</objective>
