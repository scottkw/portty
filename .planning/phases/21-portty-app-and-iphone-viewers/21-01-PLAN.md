|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-21-01 | Information disclosure | POST /api/peer/desktop/cap | high | mitigate | requireSameUser (numeric user ID, tagged and shared-in nodes refused, fail closed); POST plus a required JSON content type forces a CORS preflight the server never answers; Cache-Control no-store; nothing logged |
