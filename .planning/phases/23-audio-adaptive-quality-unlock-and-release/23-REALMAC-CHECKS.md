# Phase 23 — Real-Mac Checks (owner run sheet, 23-14)

Written before the session. Builds: the office Mac goes from its installed v1.2.x to v2.0.0-rc.1 (the rc.1 draft's arm64 DMG, M-169 part 1), then from rc.1 to rc.2 (the rc.2 draft's arm64 DMG, M-177). The home Mac keeps its installed v1.2.x until 23-16 (M-93 and M-172 step 4 need an old copy), so it is only a viewer here, in Safari, Chrome and the iPhone; it cannot stand in for the office Mac in any item.

Every row's Result cell is left empty for exactly one token: RESULT=PASS, RESULT=FAIL, RESULT=PARTIAL or RESULT=DEFERRED. A deferred item stays human_needed and is never folded into a pass. The Owner's words cell holds the owner's own reply. Each result is copied to the item's TESTING.md Last run line afterwards. M-171 and M-172 step 4 are not here; they belong to 23-16.

**Nothing written here may contain a password, a link, a token, a tailnet address or a host name.** The Mac's password is typed only into the viewer's Unlock This Mac box. Links go to a viewer by a private channel and are deleted afterwards. The soak CSV stays in the owner's own folder; only the computed values come back.

## Remote via RustDesk

The owner runs this session from home, reaching the office Mac through RustDesk.

- RustDesk captures the screen and injects input on the office Mac itself, so it can skew the two-Mac sharpness and adaptation items (M-226, M-227), M-161's CPU and drop numbers, and the soak's CPU and bandwidth numbers (M-232). During those items, close RustDesk's session, or leave it idle and minimized, and write in the Owner's words cell whether RustDesk was connected.
- Turn RustDesk's own audio off (or close the session) for the sound items (M-162, M-230), so the only sound heard on the home Mac is Portty's.
- Unlock and re-lock happen on the office Mac's real lock screen. RustDesk may show or wake it. Drive every unlock only from Portty's viewer on the home Mac or the iPhone, with RustDesk's session closed, and never type the password into RustDesk.
- Items that need someone physically at the office Mac (its physical keyboard or Caps Lock key, unplugging power, plugging in an audio device, taking over the mouse at the Mac, listening to its real speakers) are marked **Physical** below and are recorded RESULT=DEFERRED unless someone is at the Mac. M-162 counts as remote only if the sound is judged through the Portty viewer on the home Mac; otherwise it is deferred too.
- A Mac that stops answering cannot be fixed from home. Anything that can leave the office Mac without remote access (turning Portty's grants off, heavy load) waits for physical presence.

17. **M-233** — heavy load on the office Mac. Physical (the item says the owner runs it at the Mac, and a Mac that loses its tailnet under load cannot be recovered from home). DEFERRED unless someone is at the Mac. If it is run and the encoder wedges, record M-222 (optional extras).

Then the iPhone:

18. **M-175** — Home Screen link named Portty Desktop, tap to start, survives Quit All (done through RustDesk) and the upgrade (repeat its icon check right after M-177). Remote.
19. **M-228** — iPhone unmute, Unlock box above the keyboard in portrait and landscape, no save-password offer, `no_feedback` 0. Unlock from the iPhone viewer only, RustDesk closed. Remote.

Then the two Macs:

20. **M-226** — sharpness when still, smooth scrolling (through the Portty viewer's control from the home Mac), the weak link made on the home side and restored. RustDesk closed. Remote.
21. **M-227** — two viewers (home Mac Safari on a good link, iPhone on cellular), only the slow one told Slow connection. Start the moving content, then close RustDesk. Remote.

Then the upgrade and the soak:

22. **M-177** — with the desktop link open and unmuted on the home Mac and two ticking sessions on the office Mac (one with history), install the rc.2 draft's arm64 DMG over rc.1 through Portty's mover, or by the DMG replace M-177 allows if the mover refuses a same-version replace; record which. Remote. 23-15 offers the publish decision only when this row and M-232 are both RESULT=PASS.
23. **M-232** — the one-hour soak, last, on rc.2, with a viewer connected from the home Mac (Safari, unmuted) and 5 minutes of motion then 5 minutes still. Start it from a Portty terminal session on the office Mac in a scratch folder of the owner's own, then close RustDesk for the hour: `bash scripts/desktop-soak.sh --samples 62 --interval 60 --out soak.csv`. Pass rule as in the 23-11 SUMMARY (`rig/p23-rig.sh soak-verdict`). Send back the computed values, not the file. Remote.

Optional extras the owner may run while at the office Mac: M-157, M-159, M-163, M-164 (and M-222 only if a wedge shows up).
