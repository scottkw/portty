## Flagged decisions and assumptions

- **Guard (CONTEXT [Stale pgid]).** Mirror `killAdopted`'s `exited` guard: a created session gets `reaped atomic.Bool`, set right after `cmd.Wait()` returns in `ReapNaturalExit` (created branch) and in `killSession`'s own wait goroutine. `killSession` on a reaped session closes the PTY, cancels the context and returns: no group signal and no second `Wait` (RESEARCH Pitfall 5: go-pty's Wait is not for concurrent callers). Never infer "reaped" from `ExitCode() != -1` (a signal-killed child reports -1; RESEARCH Item C).
- **Seam.** The created path's two group signals go through the existing `signalGroup` seam so `recordSignals` (adopt_test.go) sees them; `killAdopted` is untouched (Chesterton: Phase 9's Pitfall 9 guard).
- **Residual window (documented, not fixed):** a dismiss between the child's exit and `ReapNaturalExit` finishing signals a zombie group, which still holds its pid, so no reuse is possible yet. Say so in the `killSession` comment.
- **Items 2 and 3: doc comments only** (no caller today, per the decision): `HubManager.Shutdown` (internal/relay/manager.go) and `closeSessionFiles` (internal/daemon/engine.go).
- **G-1 (CONTEXT post-research [Flakes]).** `TestKillClean_NoOrphans` keeps its intent (a running background job dies with its session) and its final group-gone assertion. The shell script prints a marker after starting the background job; the test waits for that marker with `testutil.WaitFor` before `Kill`, then waits with `testutil.WaitFor` (5 s) until signal 0 to the negated pgid fails. Reading the marker uses the untagged `readUntilErr` helper in native_test.go (adopt_test.go's helpers are darwin-only).
