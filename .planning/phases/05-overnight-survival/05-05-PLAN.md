<objective>
Wire the re-authentication step each guest surface runs before a redial (RESEARCH Pattern 7). The GUI peer tab re-runs the daemon's connect (new relay port after a daemon restart, refilled cap store, re-checked access); the browser asks the share server whether its cap is still good. Both map the answer to the RelayClient verdict from 05-04: redial, retry later, or stop with today's banner.

Purpose: without this, a GUI tab whose home-Mac daemon restarted dials a dead port forever, and a browser that slept through Share-off retries a revoked link forever (Pitfall 11).
Output: two mapping functions in closeReasons.ts, the App hook, the WebShareSessionView probe, tests.
</objective>

</assumption_delta_decision>

## Flagged decisions and assumptions

- [EDGE-UNCLASSIFIED SHAR-07] The spec-less edge probe could not classify SHAR-07 (status unresolved). Planner-authored edge assumptions pinned here: the home Mac's daemon restarting mid-gap (new relay port, empty cap store) reconnects; Share turned off during the gap stops the GUI and the browser without a retry loop; a tab opened before connectHost existed falls back to its peerHost.
- 'no_such_session' maps to the stopped-sharing banner, not session-ended. During a gap the daemon cannot tell "Share was turned off for this session while another stays shared" from "the session ended"; "<host> stopped sharing this session." is true in both cases. RESEARCH suggested ended; this is the planner's choice of copy that is never wrong.
- Known limit (RESEARCH Pattern 7): if the host turned Share off while the browser slept and no other session is shared, the share server's port is closed, the probe gets a network error, and the browser keeps showing "Connection lost — reconnecting…" under retry-forever. 05-07 documents it in the manual checklist.
- App's relayPort state is not updated by the hook; RelayClient keeps the fresh port. A later remount dials the stale port once, fails, and heals through the same hook.
