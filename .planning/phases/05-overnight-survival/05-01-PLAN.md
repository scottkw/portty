<objective>
Build the one replay contract every viewer (GUI tab, `portty attach`, browser) depends on: a resumable output stream. The hub counts output by frame-byte offset; a reconnecting viewer sends `?resume=<offset>` and gets only what it missed (delta) when the 256 KiB raw ring still covers it, or an explicitly marked full replay otherwise. Replays are sent in chunks of at most 16 KiB, bracketed by `MsgMeta` frames carrying `replay` and `offset`, and subscribe + capture is atomic so nothing is duplicated or lost.

Purpose: this is the research's primary recommendation ("build the resume-offset protocol in internal/relay first; every viewer depends on that one contract"). It implements the CONTEXT scrollback amendment ("GUI keeps its buffer via a resume offset: gap-only replay when the ring still covers it, else a full replay the client resets for"). The hub-owned 10,000-line history that makes the wrapped full replay real history is 05-03; liveness (pong, heartbeats) is 05-02; clients are 05-04..05-06.
Output: Hub.Join + offsets in internal/relay, all three server-side call sites (share server, local relay, viewing daemon proxy) on the new contract, old preamble tests migrated.
</objective>

## Flagged decisions and assumptions

- [EDGE-UNCLASSIFIED SHAR-07] The spec-less edge probe could not classify SHAR-07 (status unresolved). Planner-authored edge assumptions this plan pins with tests: resume == end (nothing missed) is a delta with no output; resume one byte past end, resume older than the ring start (after a wrap), an empty, negative, non-decimal or overflowing resume are all full replays; a brand-new session with no output yet sends the two meta frames and no output.
- Offsets are in frame bytes, the unit the ring already stores, because `engine.go` GetSessionTailLines/GetSessionStyledTailLines read `ScrollbackSnapshot()` as framed bytes. Replay chunks are re-framed slices of the query-stripped ring, so the frame header bytes inside the ring ride along as SOH (0x01) bytes, exactly as the single-message preamble does today. Clients take their position from the trailing offset meta, never by counting replay bytes.
- `ReconnectPreamble` is replaced by `Hub.Join`, not kept beside it, so there is one replay builder. `EnsureLiveEmulator` and `RenderSnapshot` stay exported (tests use them); Join calls their locked variants.
- Discretion: the local relay parses `resume` too, so every `/sessions/{id}/ws` endpoint accepts the same query (one line; no local client reconnects in this phase).
