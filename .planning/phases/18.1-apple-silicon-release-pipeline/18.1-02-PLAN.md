<objective>
Replace deep signing with explicit inside-out signing of every Mach-O, and give CI a signed-bundle gate that fails on unsigned code, the wrong team, a missing hardened-runtime flag, no timestamp, changed entitlements or a designated requirement that differs from v1.2's (REL-01, ROADMAP SC3). The tracer signs a scratch copy of the real arm64 bundle with the host's Developer ID identity and proves the v1.2 designated requirement comes out byte-identical: the one positive check otherwise possible only on a release tag. The expansion task pins every failure with ad-hoc fixtures and a fake codesign on PATH.

Purpose: the office Mac's Screen Recording and Accessibility grants are keyed to the designated requirement; a changed identifier or requirement silently drops them and costs a trip to the unattended Mac (CONTEXT R-2, one-way, accepted by the user with "Accept all" on 2026-11-25).
Output: scripts/sign-bundle.sh; `--signed` and `--notarized` in scripts/verify-bundle.sh; build.sh signing through both; tests in tests/verify-bundle.test.sh and tests/build-script.test.sh.
</objective>
