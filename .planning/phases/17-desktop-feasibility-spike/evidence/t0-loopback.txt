T0 tracer: synthetic frames to a pion receiver on loopback (plan 17-01)
=====================================================================

What ran: `spike selftest --out build/t0 --seconds 5` on the host. One process starts an
in-process `serve` on 127.0.0.1 (synthetic source, VideoToolbox H.264, token-gated /offer,
single UDP mux, mDNS off, no ICE servers) and a Go pion receiver that offers recvonly video
plus the "control" data channel. No Screen Recording API and no address other than
127.0.0.1 was touched.
