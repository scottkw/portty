## Flagged decisions and assumptions

- **Developer ID signing for capture rows (R10):** grants survive a same-identity rebuild; the rig's `--sign devid` build.
- **VM-only simulated lapse:** a real 30-day lapse cannot be produced in portty-test (R19 needed a disposable clone with Full Disk Access over ssh); the tag file drives the production transition and notification code, so V1 measures the real sender. Guarded by check-brand like vmpreview and vmwhois.
- **Tailnet precondition:** both VMs must be online on the tailnet with HTTPS certificates; if they are not (they are ephemeral nodes and Tailnet Lock needs the user's signature), the plan stops at Task 1's precondition and reports that a re-key is needed. That is a human action that cannot be pre-planned away.
- **Direct path is not judged here:** VMs connect through DERP (17-07 R01-vm); the direct-path check is the office-Mac M-row.
