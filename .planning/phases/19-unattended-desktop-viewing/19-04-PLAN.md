## Flagged decisions and assumptions

- **Status codes (UI-SPEC leaves them to the planner):** 401 for a rejected token and 410 for desktop off, so neither collides with the user-refusal 403 that alone carries the X-Portty marker (Phase 14 rule).
- **Check endpoint instead of reading a failed WebSocket handshake:** browsers do not expose the handshake status, so after a socket failure the viewer calls GET /desktop/check (19-06). Already-open sockets are closed with 4401/4410 by the backend (19-08).
- **Retry page reword (UI-SPEC):** a deliberate change to a pinned string; any test that pins the old body sentence is updated on purpose and named in the SUMMARY.
