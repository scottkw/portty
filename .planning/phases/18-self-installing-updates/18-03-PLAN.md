<objective>
Make a broken update undo itself (UPD-07). If N+1 never reports healthy, or starts and dies, the installer stops only N+1's GUI, puts N back atomically and relaunches it; the relaunched N keeps whichever daemon owns the sessions (a committed N+1 daemon cannot hand sessions back to an older release) and tells the user the update did not take. Every relaunch retries the `open` LaunchServices refuses right after the old GUI exits (row A9). Prove both rollback cases, a refused swap, a hung old GUI and the relaunch retry in the VM.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC2; CONTEXT [Rollback] and the [Installer] relaunch note (A9); reliability first (the remote Mac is unattended).
Output: rollback and the relaunch retry in RunInstaller, realGUIPids and Signal wiring, Service.ShowResult, consumeInstallResult with the keep-daemon ensure, VM-only fault hooks, rows R2-A..R2-D and A9-fix.
</objective>
