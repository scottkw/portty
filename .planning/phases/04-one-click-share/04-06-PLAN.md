## Phase Goal

Sharing is one on/off switch per session: shared means tailnet peers and a tailnet web link at once, always full control, with a clean stop and CLI attach from any terminal. Funnel and read-only links are gone. (ROADMAP Phase 4 goal, verbatim.)

This last plan finishes the viewing side in the GUI and the browser, deletes the join-code dialog, adds the removal gate that proves SC4 and runs in CI, refreshes the docs, and runs the full phase gate. The real two-Mac flow can't run in the Tart VM (no Tailscale), so it becomes manual item M-80 for the deferred UAT; everything else is proven by tests with fakes or in the VM with the vmpreview build.
