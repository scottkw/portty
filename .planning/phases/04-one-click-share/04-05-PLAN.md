## Phase Goal

Sharing is one on/off switch per session: shared means tailnet peers and a tailnet web link at once, always full control, with a clean stop and CLI attach from any terminal. Funnel and read-only links are gone. (ROADMAP Phase 4 goal, verbatim.)

This plan puts the switch in the GUI and deletes the old sharing UI. The toolbar Share button opens a small popover with one switch; the sidebar menu gains Share / Stop Sharing; Settings ▸ Sharing shrinks to status plus a sentence. Every Funnel, read-only, QR, share-warning and web-server control, stub, style and test goes. Peer tabs and the web page come in 04-06. Sequential plans; green at the boundary.
