## Phase Goal

Users navigate everything from tabs plus a sidebar: the Hub grid is gone, and the sidebar lists local sessions and tailnet peers' sessions, each opening in a tab. (ROADMAP Goal, verbatim.)

This plan delivers the banner part of ROADMAP success criterion 8 (LOOK-01) and fixes the UI-REVIEW 01 banner findings: hex colours, the ⚠ glyph and text × dismiss buttons. It adds the Tailscale banner's "Open Tailscale" action (UI-SPEC "Banner") without breaking NET-01's "never installs or configures Tailscale".

RESEARCH Open Question 5 is resolved as specced (`open -a Tailscale` with a URL fallback).
Output: the OpenTailscale binding with Go tests, unified banners, a token-only App.tsx, and TESTING.md.
</objective>
