<objective>
Put the share on the shared Mac's always-visible surface: the GUI's liveness stream to the daemon, the tray slot (Share Desktop…, Desktop shared with N, Stop Sharing) and the tray icon variant while anyone watches.

Purpose: DSHR-06 asks for a persistent "shared with N" indicator with one-click stop; the tray is on screen whenever the GUI process lives, and the stream that feeds it is what ends the share when the GUI goes away.
Output: app_desktop_share.go (events loop and tray handlers), tray edits, two icon images, tests. Wails bindings, notifications and the window-forward rules are 22-09.
</objective>
