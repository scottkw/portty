CONTEXT Who may join (second bullet, costly, accepted; settled in CONTEXT), [Grants] (memory only, one active share), [Media] (PeerConnection only after admission; the remote-address check is implemented by node identity per the After-research bullet "media is tied to the guest's node identity, not address strings"), [Admit] (own account on a guest link follows Admit), [Signalling] (admission messages, additive), Claude's Discretion (5-minute timeout, 1 per user and node, 8 per share, denied cannot knock again) and After research (2 pending per user, deny and revoke keyed by user ID), RESEARCH Pattern 3, Finding 8, Pitfall 8.

Planner notes (reversible): close codes 4430 denied, 4431 admit_timeout, 4432 revoked, 4433 share_ended sit beside the existing 4401, 4410 and 4429; a guest's token failure at join uses 4401 with reason token_invalid; request IDs are 16 random hex characters; the guest hello carries the same caps as an own viewer, so the page opens its input channel with its offer (after admission) and the rights gate drops input until a grant.
