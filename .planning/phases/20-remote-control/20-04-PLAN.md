<objective>
Make a window share safe and usable (DCTL-05): the shared window is raised when controlled, keys and clicks reach only that window, clicks never land outside it or on anything covering it, and the system chords cannot escape it.

Purpose: CGEvent posts are global, so without these gates a viewer typing into a shared window could type into another app it cannot see (research PITFALLS 11, quoted in CONTEXT).
Output: window.go and the AX/CGWindowList bridge.

Decisions implemented: CONTEXT Input security boundaries (window share gate, raise-then-drop, clamp, drop outside), [Window] (raise by AX plus app activation, no private API; extended to the research ladder), [Testing]; Claude's Discretion after research (topmost check on every click; Cmd+Tab and Cmd+Space disabled in window shares).
</objective>
